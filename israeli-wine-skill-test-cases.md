# Israeli Wine Skill Test Cases

Use these questions to verify that an Israeli wine agent skill combines static domain knowledge with dynamic lookup behavior from Vivino or other current wine sources when relevant.

## Questions

1. What are the main Israeli wine regions, and how do their climates affect the styles of wine produced there?

2. Compare the Golan Heights, Judean Hills, Galilee, and Negev as wine regions. Which grape varieties tend to perform best in each?

3. I like full-bodied Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah. Which Israeli wineries and bottles should I explore, and what current Vivino ratings or prices can you find?

4. What are the most important grape varieties grown in Israel, including both international varieties and local or revived varieties?

5. Recommend Israeli wines for someone who enjoys Burgundy-style Pinot Noir or Chardonnay. Include winery examples and explain where in Israel these styles are most likely to succeed.

6. Which Israeli wineries are considered leading producers today, and what are they best known for?

7. Find highly rated Israeli red wines on Vivino, summarize their ratings, price ranges, regions, and producer styles, and mention any uncertainty in the data.

8. What Israeli wines pair well with shakshuka, grilled lamb, sabich, and spicy Middle Eastern salads?

9. Explain the history of modern Israeli wine, from Carmel and Baron Edmond de Rothschild to boutique wineries and current premium producers.

10. I am visiting Israel and want to plan a wine-focused trip. Which regions, wineries, and tasting routes should I prioritize for a three-day itinerary?

11. Who are the main producers of orange wines in Israel, and what styles do they make?

12. Recommend cheap but still okay Israeli wine for a picnic. Include options that are easy to find in Israel and explain why they fit picnic drinking.

13. What are the best wine bars in Tel Aviv right now, and what makes each one worth visiting?

14. What are the best wines from Tzora Vineyards, and how do they differ by vineyard, blend, or vintage?

15. Which wine region in Israel is most similar to Argentina, especially for someone who likes Malbec, altitude, sun, and bold red wines?

16. What are the best white wines in Israel, including premium examples and good-value bottles?

17. Recommend a good Israeli wine from a local or revived variety up to 100 shekels.

## Expected Coverage

- Static knowledge: regions, climate, soils, grape varieties, winery history, major producers, orange wine, local varieties, food pairings, and travel context.
- Dynamic knowledge: current Vivino ratings, availability, prices, vintages, wine bars, and links when the user asks for recommendations or market data.
- Source behavior: cite current external sources for live data and clearly distinguish static wine knowledge from changing marketplace information.
